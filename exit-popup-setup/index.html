Exit-Intent Email Capture — Setup Guide

A popup that catches visitors leaving your Page 2 (the product page) without buying. Instead of losing them, you capture their email and send them a free guide. One file to install, works on all your funnels.

What it does

When it fires

  • Desktop: When the visitor moves their mouse toward the browser's address bar or close button (exit intent). This is the universal signal that someone is about to leave.
  • Mobile: When the visitor scrolls past 40% of the page, then stops scrolling for 3 seconds. Mobile doesn't have mouse-leave, so this is the equivalent — they've read enough to know what it is, but they've stalled.

It won't fire until the visitor has been on the page for at least 4 seconds, so nobody gets hit the instant they land.

It shows once per visitor. If they close it or submit, it won't come back (saved in their browser).

What the visitor sees

Preview — desktop

40 medications and 19 doctors later, we learned 3 things.

I put them in a short guide so you don't have to piece it together the way my mother did. It's free.

Your email address
Send Me the Guide
No spam. Unsubscribe anytime.

Matches your funnel's green/navy color scheme. Fades in with a slide-up animation. Closes on the X, clicking outside, or pressing Escape.

What happens on submit

  • The email is sent to your GoHighLevel webhook (you set this up in Step 2 below).
  • A Lead event fires on your Meta Pixel so the signup shows in your ad reporting.
  • The visitor sees "Check your inbox — it's on the way." and the popup closes after 3.5 seconds.
Setup — 3 steps
1 Add the script to your GHL page

Open your Page 2 in GoHighLevel's page editor. Go to Settings → Custom Code → Body (Footer) and paste this one line:

<script src="exit-popup.js"></script>

Then upload the exit-popup.js file to the same page's media/assets so the script can find it. If GHL doesn't let you host JS files directly, paste the entire contents of exit-popup.js inside <script>...</script> tags in the footer instead.

Done when: you preview the GHL page, move your mouse to the top of the screen, and the popup appears.
2 Connect your GHL webhook so emails land in your CRM

Inside GoHighLevel:

  • Go to Automation → Workflows and create a new workflow.
  • Set the trigger to Inbound Webhook. GHL gives you a webhook URL.
  • Copy that URL.

Open exit-popup.js in a text editor and find this line near the top:

formEndpoint: 'https://YOUR-GHL-WEBHOOK-URL-HERE.com/submit',

Replace the placeholder URL with your real GHL webhook URL. Save the file.

Done when: you test the popup, enter a fake email, and it appears as a new contact in GHL.
3 Set up the auto-delivery so they get the guide

Still in the same GHL workflow from Step 2:

  • After the webhook trigger, add an action: Send Email.
  • Subject line: something like "Here's the guide I mentioned"
  • Body: a short message from Ethan with a link to download the PDF guide (host it in GHL's file manager or a Google Drive link).
  • Optional: add a 2-day delay, then a second email nudging them back to the product page.
The lead magnet: You need a short PDF guide to deliver. The popup promises "the 3 things I learned after 40 medications and 19 doctors." A 2-3 page PDF pulling those 3 lessons from your story would work. We can build this together when you're ready — just let me know.
Done when: you test the full flow — popup → enter email → email arrives in your inbox with the guide link.

Customizing the copy

Open exit-popup.js and edit the config block at the top:

var CONFIG = {
  headline:       '40 medications and 19 doctors later, we learned 3 things.',
  subheadline:    'I put them in a short guide so you don\'t have to piece it together the way my mother did. It\'s free.',
  leadMagnetName: 'The PANDAS Parent Guide',
  buttonText:     'Send Me the Guide',
  privacyNote:    'No spam. Unsubscribe anytime.',
  formEndpoint:   'https://YOUR-GHL-WEBHOOK-URL-HERE.com/submit',
  successMessage: 'Check your inbox — it\'s on the way.',
  storageKey:     'gvn_exit_popup_shown',
  delayMs:        4000
};

Change any text you want. The delayMs controls how many milliseconds the visitor has to be on the page before the popup can fire (4000 = 4 seconds).

To reset the popup during testing (so it shows again after you've already seen it), open your browser's developer tools, go to Application → Local Storage, and delete the gvn_exit_popup_shown key.
