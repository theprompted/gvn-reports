Your 12 Founder Videos

Twelve videos, twelve scripts. Each one opens on you talking to the camera with a headline on screen, like the previews further down. Same setup for all twelve: you, your iPhone, walking around the farm.

Make these yours

Edit the scripts as you go. Change any line so it rolls off your tongue. Make them longer, make them shorter. Feel it out.

The scripts are there to give you some clay to mold, so you don't have to start from a blank page. Don't feel boxed in by them.

What happens after you film

Once you're done with all twelve clips and we know exactly what you said in each one, we build a landing page for each video. Each page picks up right where its video leaves off, so the story stays the same from the ad all the way to checkout.

Every first page stays compliant, the same as your videos: no CBD or other flagged words up front. That way each funnel is congruent from the first second of the video to the order button.

How to film them
  1. Pick a teleprompter app below and load a script.
  2. Hold the phone vertical at eye level, front camera on. A selfie stick or gimbal helps if you have one.
  3. Walk slowly around the farm. Morning or late-afternoon light, with the sun in front of you, not behind.
  4. Change up the spot for each video, like the previews do, so the twelve don't all look the same.
  5. Clip on a wireless mic if you have one. Wind and footsteps are the biggest sound problem outside.
  6. Turn on Do Not Disturb. Read each script start to finish. If you trip on a line, pause and say it again. We cut the mistakes out in editing.
  7. Plan on an hour or two for all twelve, then send us the raw files.
