The one thing you need to do

The buttons point at placeholders right now. They look like this:

#REPLACE-WITH-1-BOTTLE-CHECKOUT-URL
#REPLACE-WITH-3-BOTTLE-CHECKOUT-URL
#REPLACE-WITH-6-BOTTLE-CHECKOUT-URL

They're obvious on purpose. A wrong variant ID would quietly send buyers to the wrong product and you wouldn't catch it until you looked at the orders. A link that visibly does nothing is the safer failure.

Why they aren't filled in

I went to wire them up and checked your live store first. Here's what's actually there:

What the page sellsWhat exists on the store
1 month — 1 bottle — $69One variant, 52672665420098, at $69.99
3 months — 3 bottles — $149Doesn't exist
6 months — 6 bottles — $269Doesn't exist

I checked all 48 products in the catalogue. There's no kids-oil bundle of any size, and the $149 / $269 price points don't appear anywhere. So there was nothing real to link to — the bundles the page sells aren't products yet.

How to fix it

  1. Create the 3-pack and 6-pack in Shopify at $149 and $269.
    The pattern already exists in your store — your gummies use one product with a "Stock Up and Save" option (Single jar / 2 jars / 3 jars), each its own variant. Copy that. One product with three variants is cleaner than three separate products.
  2. Get each variant ID. Open https://www.greenvalleynutrition.com/products/<your-handle>.json and read the variants array — each entry has an id.
  3. Build the cart links: 
    https://www.greenvalleynutrition.com/cart/<VARIANT_ID>:1
  4. Find and replace each placeholder with its real link. Six per file, three files.

If you want the buttons live before the bundles exist

You can point all three at the existing single bottle with a quantity:

https://www.greenvalleynutrition.com/cart/52672665420098:1
https://www.greenvalleynutrition.com/cart/52672665420098:3
https://www.greenvalleynutrition.com/cart/52672665420098:6

I tested this and it works — :3 puts three bottles in a real cart. But it charges 3 × $69.99 = $209.97, not the $149 the page promises. Only use it as a stopgap, and change the prices on the page to match if you do.
